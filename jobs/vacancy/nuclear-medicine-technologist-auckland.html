Nuclear Medicine Technologist — Auckland

PermanentFull-timeAuckland, New Zealand

Ethicare is delighted to be working with Health New Zealand | Te Whatu Ora to recruit a Nuclear Medicine Technologist for a leading public radiology service in Auckland. You’ll join an experienced team of physicians and technologists delivering high-quality diagnostic and therapeutic procedures, including a nationally recognised radionuclide therapy service.

About the role

You’ll perform a wide range of nuclear medicine procedures, prepare and administer radiopharmaceuticals, and carry out quality-control protocols, working with modern SPECT/CT gamma-camera technology. You’ll also contribute to a national PRRT radionuclide therapy service, within a collaborative team that values teamwork, adaptability and clinical excellence.

What the role involves

  • Performing a wide range of nuclear medicine procedures
  • Preparing and administering radiopharmaceuticals
  • Conducting quality-control protocols
  • Contributing to a national radionuclide (PRRT) therapy service
  • Working with modern SPECT/CT gamma-camera technology

About you

  • A tertiary qualification in Nuclear Medicine
  • Current registration, or eligibility, with the New Zealand MRT Board
  • A valid Annual Practising Certificate in Nuclear Medicine
  • A proactive, team-oriented approach

About Auckland

Auckland is New Zealand’s largest and most diverse city, wrapped around two harbours, with islands, black-sand west-coast surf beaches and vineyards all within easy reach. Alongside the widest range of specialist roles in the country, it offers a genuinely international city life and easy escapes at the weekend.
