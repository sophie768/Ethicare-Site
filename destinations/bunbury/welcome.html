Welcome to Bunbury

Bunbury is the city that makes the South West work: Western Australia’s largest regional centre, two hours south of Perth, with a harbour, an estuary and a surf coast inside the same city limits. Here it is in nine numbers, then six things nobody tells you until you have moved.

A working port that turned outward

Around 75,000 people live across Greater Bunbury, on a stretch of coast between the Indian Ocean and the Leschenault Estuary. It grew up as a port — mineral sands, alumina and woodchip still move through it — and that industrial spine is why the city has infrastructure a place this size would not otherwise justify: a regional hospital, a university campus, a theatre, and a farmers’ market people drive an hour to reach.

For clinicians the proposition is specific. The region’s main public hospital is the South West’s referral centre, taking work from district hospitals across a region roughly the size of Belgium. Alongside it sits a genuine private sector, including the only PET service anywhere in the South West. You get regional breadth without being remote: Perth is a two-hour drive, not a flight.

The honest costs: a smaller specialist bench than a capital, a referral list that runs upward to Perth for the most complex work, and a rental market that tightens fast when a big project staffs up. This guide prices all three plainly.

Six things you only learn once you are here

The geography does the work
Surf beach, sheltered bay and estuary are all inside the city. Which one you use depends on the wind, and you learn that in a fortnight.
Dolphins are residents, not an attraction
A wild population lives in Koombana Bay year-round. People stop noticing them, which is the most Bunbury thing about it.
It is a referral centre, not a district hospital
Work arrives from towns across the South West. The case mix is wider than the city’s own population would suggest.
Wine country is a weeknight, not a weekend
The Ferguson Valley is half an hour inland. Margaret River is the trip you plan; Ferguson is the one you just do.
Winter is genuinely wet
The South West gets far more rain than Perth. People arriving from a dry climate are caught out by their first July.
Housing moves with industry
When a major project staffs up, rentals tighten quickly. Timing matters more here than in a big city.
A note on Country
  • Bunbury sits on Wardandi Noongar country, in the south-west corner of the Noongar nation. The Noongar name for this place is Goomburrup.
  • Acknowledgement of Country opens meetings and events across the region, and taking the time to learn whose Country you are on is genuinely appreciated here.
