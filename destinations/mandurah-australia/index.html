Moving to Mandurah

A guide for healthcare professionals · Nine chapters

A waterside city on the Peel estuary, under an hour south of Perth by road or rail. Once a holiday and retirement town, Mandurah is now one of Western Australia’s fastest-growing cities and a genuine option for a clinician who wants the coast without the capital’s prices. This guide covers what is specific to Mandurah; everything national is explained once, elsewhere, and linked from here.
