  gorge walls, tropical woodland above. **Queensland, confirmed.** It reads strongly as Wallaman

## Bunbury note

`bunbury/bunbury-suburban-playground-golden-hour.jpg` — a suburban playground at golden hour,
**Bunbury WA**, confirmed. Filed under its true location, but used on the national
`/australia` page in the "Can my family come too?" section with a deliberately generic caption
(no suburb named), which is honest: the filename records where it is, the caption claims nothing.

- `qld-tidal-creek-mouth-swimmers.jpg` — a tidal creek mouth on a humid overcast day: sandbars,
  families swimming in calm shallow water, mangroves and coastal heath behind, a rock groyne at
  right. Filed to Queensland on Sophie's attribution ("Brisbane"), which is taken as the **region**,
  not the city — this is plainly not central Brisbane.
  **No landmark is visible, so the exact place is unverified.** It reads as a South East Queensland
  creek mouth of the Tallebudgera / Currumbin / Currimundi type, all inside a couple of hours of the
  city, but the vegetation and sand would look much the same in northern New South Wales.
  Caption it for **what it shows** — a calm creek mouth an easy drive from the city, the kind of
  water you take small children to — and do not name the creek. Note the library already holds
  `qld-aerial-views-of-tallebudgera-creek-2665579873.jpg`; if this is the same place at ground
  level, someone who knows it will recognise it, and then it can be renamed.
  Genuinely useful either way: every other coastal shot in the Australian library is open surf, and
  "somewhere safe to swim with a toddler" is a real question for a relocating family.

- `qld-mareeba-mosque.jpg` — Mareeba Mosque: red brick and white render, a single minaret, blue
  arched windows, the bismillah and its English translation over the entrance, a neighbouring
  weatherboard house with a green tin roof, mown lawn, hard blue sky. Queensland, on Sophie's
  attribution; the building is distinctive enough that it is safely captioned as Mareeba.

  **The most useful photograph added this session for the belonging argument**, and the hardest kind
  to source. The Australia country page's belonging section says moving country means rebuilding
  "a place to worship", and that whatever you are looking for "almost certainly exists — the useful
  question is which city". Until now there was no photograph anywhere in the library that evidenced
  that. This one does it better than a capital-city mosque would: Mareeba is a small town on the
  Atherton Tablelands, and it shows that a settled faith community exists in **regional** Queensland,
  not only in Sydney or Melbourne. For a Muslim clinician weighing a regional post — where much of
  the genuine opportunity is — that is a materially different message.

  Also worth noting for what it is not: an unremarkable suburban building on an ordinary street,
  photographed plainly. That is the point. It says "this is normal here", which no stock image of a
  grand mosque could.
