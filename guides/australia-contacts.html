Official links & everyday contacts

The official websites, in one place

A handful of trustworthy, official sources for the practical questions: registration, visas, healthcare, tax, schools and driver licences. Many things in Australia run nationally through Ahpra and the federal government, but a few, schools, licences and tenancy, are run by each state and territory.

Run by the states

Schools, licences & tenancy, by state

A few of the things you’ll need are run by each state and territory rather than nationally, finding a school and checking its zone, swapping your overseas driver licence, and your rights as a renter. Open the one for where you’re heading.

New South Wales

Schools & enrolment zones: NSW Department of Education · Driver licences: Service NSW · Renting: NSW Fair Trading.

Victoria

Schools & zones: Victorian Department of Education · Driver licences: VicRoads · Renting: Consumer Affairs Victoria.

Queensland

Schools & catchments: Queensland Department of Education · Driver licences: Queensland Transport (TMR) · Renting: Residential Tenancies Authority.

Western Australia

Schools & local-intake areas: WA Department of Education · Driver licences: Department of Transport WA · Renting: Consumer Protection WA.

South Australia

Schools & zones: SA Department for Education · Driver licences: Service SA · Renting: Consumer & Business Services SA.

Tasmania

Schools & intake areas: Tasmanian Department for Education · Driver licences: Transport Tasmania · Renting: Consumer, Building & Occupational Services.

Australian Capital Territory

Schools & priority enrolment areas: ACT Education Directorate · Driver licences: Access Canberra · Renting: ACT Government tenancy information.

Northern Territory

Schools & enrolment: NT Department of Education · Driver licences: Motor Vehicle Registry (MVR) · Renting: NT Consumer Affairs.

These are the departments to search for in each state. Names occasionally change, and several have an online school-finder that maps the catchment for any address. Tell us where you’re heading and we’ll point you to the current pages and, for schools, the right starting point for your visa type.

Multicultural bodies, by state

Each state and territory has a multicultural commission and a network of migrant resource centres, a good first call for local groups, events and settlement help. A starting point, not an exhaustive list.

New South Wales

Multicultural NSW · Settlement Services International · SydWest & other regional migrant resource centres.

Victoria

Victorian Multicultural Commission · AMES Australia · Ethnic Communities’ Council of Victoria.

Queensland

Multicultural Australia · Ethnic Communities Council of Queensland · regional migrant settlement services.

Western Australia

Office of Multicultural Interests (WA) · Metropolitan Migrant Resource Centre · Ethnic Communities Council of WA.

South Australia

Multicultural Affairs SA · Australian Migrant Resource Centre · Ethnic Communities Council of SA.

Tasmania

Multicultural Council of Tasmania · Migrant Resource Centre Tasmania (north & south).

ACT

ACT Office of Multicultural Affairs · Companion House · Migrant & Refugee Settlement Services of the ACT.

Northern Territory

Multicultural Council of the Northern Territory · Melaleuca Australia · local settlement services.

Links open external sites we don’t control. The state bodies are examples to help you start; availability changes over time, so if you can’t find yours, just ask, we’ll be honest about the makeup of any town we place people in.
