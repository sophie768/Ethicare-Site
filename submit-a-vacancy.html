What happens next

After you hit submit

No auto-responder, no silence. A person reads what you send and replies — usually within a working day — with an honest answer before either of us spends real time.

01

A person reads it

Within a working day: whether it’s a role we can fill, and a realistic timeline. If it isn’t right for us, we’ll say so plainly.

02

A short conversation

We talk through the service, the team and the constraints — supervision, budget and what the role really needs.

03

Search & prepare

A targeted search, then the checks that matter: where each candidate stands with the board, the visa route, and whether the move is realistic for their family.

04

Shortlist, offer, arrival

A short, relevant shortlist for your process; then offer, registration and relocation held in one place, with support through the first year.

One person stays accountable from your first message to the clinician’s first year. New to hiring internationally? The recruitment toolkit walks through what it takes — or, if you’d rather talk it through before writing anything, start a conversation.
