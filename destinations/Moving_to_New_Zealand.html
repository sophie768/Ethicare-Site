Welcome

Most of what you need to know isn’t about a city.

It’s about a country. New Zealand runs one health system, one tax system, one set of tenancy rules and one school framework from Kaitaia to Bluff. Learn them once and they hold wherever you end up.

That’s what this guide is for. It’s the layer underneath the regional guides — the systems, the money, the paperwork and the rhythms of the year. Dry-sounding subjects, mostly, but they’re the ones that decide whether your first six months feel manageable or bewildering.

Read it in order or dip in. Nothing here is trying to sell you a move — it’s here so you can make the decision with your eyes open.

How this guide works

Two layers, and you’ll want both

We split the writing so you never read the same thing twice. This guide holds the country. Each regional guide holds the place.

Layer one · you are here

The national guide

The systems that don’t change when you cross a regional boundary. How healthcare is funded and organised. Tax, IRD numbers and KiwiSaver. Tenancy law and Healthy Homes. Licence conversion. School years and NCEA. Bringing a dog.

Fifteen chapters. Roughly two hours end to end, and you only ever read it once.

Layer two · pick one

The regional guides

Everything that is true of one place and nowhere else. Which hospital, and what it’s like. Suburb by suburb with real commute times and real rents. School zones. Where clinicians actually live. What to do on your first free Saturday.

Thirteen regional guides, nine chapters each. All written in full.

If you already know where you’re going, start with that region’s guide and come back here when the practical questions arrive — because they will, usually about three weeks in.

Sophie’s insight

I moved to New Zealand in my twenties and stayed for years — Wellington first, then Queenstown. What nobody told me is that the systems are the easy part. They’re small, sensible and quick to learn. The part that takes real effort is building a life a long way from the one you had. Give that the same planning you’d give the paperwork.

Sophie CareemFounder & CEO, Ethicare Resourcing
