Applying through Ethicare

How we support you when you apply through us

The guides and tools are free whoever you end up working for. Apply through us and one person supports you personally, at no cost to you, and nothing goes to an employer without your agreement.

  1. We get to know youYour goals, your family and whether Australia or New Zealand is the right fit, talked through before anything else.
  2. We help with registrationWhat your registration needs, and help getting the application right first time.
  3. We find roles and prepare youLocations, employers and pay talked through honestly, then preparation for each interview.
  4. We help you weigh the offerWe go through the offer with you, so the decision is yours and well informed.
  5. We support the moveHelp with the visa steps where we can, and the practical side of moving your life overseas.
  6. We stay in touch after you arriveContact as you settle into your new life and career, long after the first day.

Employers make hiring decisions, and the relevant authorities decide registration and visas. We help you through the process but cannot guarantee those outcomes. Immigration New Zealand and the Department of Home Affairs publish the rules that apply to you in full.
