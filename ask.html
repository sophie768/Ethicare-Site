Ask Ethicare

What’s on your mind?

It answers from our own guides, then points you at the page you needed.

Most people arrive here with a question that doesn’t fit a menu — whether their registration will transfer, what the money really looks like, whether a partner can work. Ask it the way you’d ask a person who has done this before.

AI-assisted, checked against official sources — regulators and immigration authorities make the final decisions. Please don’t include passport, financial or other sensitive personal information.

What it’s good at, and what it won’t decide

Worth asking

  • Which registration route applies to your profession, and the order the steps come in
  • What a salary figure actually means once tax, super or KiwiSaver and the cost of living are in it
  • Which city or region fits the life you’re describing
  • What a partner or your children can do on the visa you’d be on
  • Which of our guides answers the thing you’re stuck on — there are several hundred pages here
  • How the two countries differ on a specific point, rather than in general

Not its job

  • Telling you that you will be registered, or that a visa will be granted. Regulators and immigration authorities decide that, and only they can
  • Quoting the pay for a specific vacancy
  • Immigration advice, legal advice or financial advice
  • Anything that turns on your own documents — that is a conversation with a person
  • Guaranteeing a requirement won’t change. They do change, which is why it sends you to the official source

It is an AI assistant, and we’d rather say so plainly than let you find out. Your question and the conversation you have with it are sent to a specialist AI provider to generate the answer. We do not store the conversation and it is not added to any record about you — so please don’t put passport numbers, financial details or medical information into it. How we use your information.

If it gets something wrong, we want to know. Tell us and we’ll correct the page it drew on rather than defend it — talk to the team.
