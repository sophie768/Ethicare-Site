Where clinicians live

Bunbury Guide · Chapter 5 of 9

Bunbury is small enough that nowhere is a bad commute, which frees you to choose on lifestyle rather than logistics. The real decision is which water you want to be near.

Make one decision first

Before you look at a single listing: fix your work site. The city’s clinical sites are not all in the same place, and while nothing here is more than about fifteen minutes from anything else, the difference between a five-minute and a twenty-minute drive is worth knowing before you sign a lease rather than after.

Then choose your side. Ocean side means surf, wind and sunsets. Bay side means flat water, the port and the middle of town. Estuary and inland means the newest family housing and the most space for the money.

The areas

The city centre & Marlston
Apartments and townhouses, walking distance to the waterfront, restaurants and the entertainment centre. Suits people arriving alone or as a couple who want to be able to walk somewhere on a Friday. Least space per dollar.
South Bunbury & Withers
Established, close to the ocean beaches and to schools. Housing stock varies street by street — this is the area where it is most worth walking around before committing.
East Bunbury & College Grove
Quieter, greener, close to the university campus. Popular with families who want to be near good schools without paying a beachfront premium.
Dalyellup
South along the coast, newer estates, a young-family demographic and a genuine sense of community. A slightly longer run into town, and the trade most people are happy with.
Australind & Eaton
North across the estuary. The most space and the newest housing for the money, with the estuary rather than the surf on your doorstep. A ten to fifteen-minute drive in.
Gelorup & the rural fringe
Larger blocks, trees, and the closest thing to a small-holding within commuting distance. Suits people who want land and do not mind a car being essential.

Renting first is the sensible order

Almost everyone we place rents for six to twelve months before buying, and here there is a specific reason to. Bunbury is a working port in a resources state, and the rental market tightens quickly when a major project staffs up. Arriving with something secured for your first month — even short-term — takes the pressure off the decision that matters more.

How leases, bonds, applications and tenancy rights work is the same across Australia and is covered once in Moving to Australia. Nothing about renting in Bunbury is legally different; it is the timing and the supply that are local.

Practical, in order
  • Fix your work site before you choose a suburb.
  • Decide your water: ocean, bay, or estuary and inland.
  • Rent for the first six to twelve months, and secure something before you fly.
  • Walk the street at the time of day you would be coming home.
  • If schools matter, check catchments before you sign — they do not always follow the suburb boundary.
The short version

Nowhere in Bunbury is a difficult commute, so choose on the life rather than the drive. Ocean side for surf, bay side for the middle of town, north across the estuary for space — and rent first, because supply here moves with industry rather than with the season.
