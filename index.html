What Ethicare does

A new role. A new home. A life that feels right for you.

Ethicare Resourcing helps healthcare professionals find their next role and make a new home in New Zealand or Australia. We know a move like this is about much more than a job. It is about your future, the people you love and the life you hope to build. We believe everyone deserves clear information, useful tools and someone to talk things through with. That is why we are here: to help you explore what is possible, decide what feels right for you and support you through the move.

    Common questions

    Still figuring things out?

    You don’t need to have all the answers yet. Here are some of the questions we’re asked most often.

    I’m only thinking about moving. Is it too early to speak to Ethicare?

    Not at all. You don’t need to have chosen a country, started registration, or decided you’re definitely moving.

    Most people arrive with the same questions in roughly the same order: can I register, what would I earn, what does it cost to live there, where would we live, and what roles exist. You can work through those here, and change your mind at any point.

    Can you tell me whether I’m likely to be eligible for registration before I apply?

    Yes. Our pathway checker helps you understand the likely registration route for your profession, and where you may need to look into things further.

    It’s designed as a starting point, not a substitute for a formal decision from the relevant registration authority.

    Should I travel to New Zealand as a visitor and look for a job when I arrive?

    We strongly advise against relocating to New Zealand as a visitor in the hope of finding a healthcare job once you arrive.

    We see this more often than we would like: professionals spend thousands on flights, accommodation, registration and living costs — sometimes bringing their families — believing that being in New Zealand will make it easier to secure a role. It rarely does. Being registered, or physically in the country, doesn’t guarantee employment: demand changes, some professions are far more competitive than others, and employers may have few vacancies or recruit directly.

    Our advice is simple: secure the right role first, then plan the move. New Zealand can offer an incredible life, but the move should be built around a realistic employment pathway, not the hope that something will turn up.

    Why are you currently focusing on medical imaging roles in Australia?

    We work in the areas where we have strong employer relationships and specialist recruitment experience. In Australia, that’s currently medical imaging — radiographers, mammographers, sonographers and other imaging professionals.

    We’d rather offer genuine expertise and realistic opportunities in a smaller number of professions than advertise roles we can’t properly support. We expect to broaden this over time, and you can tell us your profession so we can let you know when it opens.

    Do I need to have found my role through Ethicare to use your tools and resources?

    No. The tools, guides and resources are free to use whether you find a role through Ethicare, apply directly to an employer, or are simply exploring your options.

    If you do decide you’d like our help finding the right role, you can register your interest with us.

    Why aren’t there many nursing vacancies in New Zealand?

    The New Zealand nursing market has changed considerably. Many hospitals recruit nurses directly, and there are currently internationally qualified nurses who have completed New Zealand registration but still found it difficult to secure suitable roles.

    For that reason, we don’t want to encourage people to spend significant amounts of money on registration or relocation simply on the assumption that a job will be available.

    Should I complete the nursing OSCE before looking for a job in New Zealand?

    We’d be very cautious about spending significant money on the OSCE and associated registration costs without first understanding your realistic employment prospects.

    Completing registration doesn’t guarantee a job. Where possible, we recommend exploring employer demand and your individual prospects before committing to substantial costs. If you’re weighing this up, talk to us first.

    Do you charge healthcare professionals for helping them find a job?

    No. We don’t charge candidates a recruitment fee for securing a role through Ethicare. Where we recruit on behalf of an employer, our fee is paid by the employer.

    You may still have your own costs — registration, visas, medicals, documentation or relocation — and we’ll try to make those clear rather than pretend the move is free.

    Still not sure? Ask Ethicare a question in your own words, or talk to the team.
