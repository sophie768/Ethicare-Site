Australia · what we’re building next

More Australian professions are in development

We opened in Australia with medical imaging because we understand it deeply — the registration, the pay, the questions people actually ask. We’re building the same depth for more professions, rather than publishing thin pages just to say they’re here.

We’re starting with what we know really well

In New Zealand we recruit across most of healthcare. Australia is younger for us — we opened here in March 2026 — so we’re growing it one profession at a time, and we’d rather tell you honestly where each one stands than pretend it’s ready.

Live now: medical imaging & radiation — sonography, radiography, radiation therapy, nuclear medicine and radiology, each with a full pathway, indicative pay and the honest picture of the move.

In the works

In development right now

Each of these is a genuinely different registration picture — often more than one regulator inside a single family. That’s exactly why we’re taking the time: a guide that gets the route wrong costs you months.

  • Nursing & midwiferyIn development

    The largest registered workforce in the country, and the one with the most ways in. We’re mapping the Nursing and Midwifery Board route properly — outcomes, bridging, the lot — before we publish it.

  • Mental healthIn development

    Psychology, mental-health nursing and the wider team — each answering to a different regulator and a different route. Worth getting right rather than quick.

  • Theatre & perioperativeIn development

    Scrub, scout, anaesthetic and recovery roles across public and private lists. The registration picture differs by role, so we’re building it role by role.

  • Allied healthIn development

    Physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech pathology and more — several regulators, several routes. A broad family, and we’re taking it one profession at a time.

Not sure your route even works?Our pathway checker already covers more than twenty professions — well beyond the ones we recruit into — so you can get an honest answer on registration today, whether or not the full guide is live.
