# NT photography — audit

## The lesson (23 Aug 2026)

`nt-uluru.jpg` **was Parliament House, Canberra.** It ran as the hero photograph *and* the
og:image of the Australia destinations hub, captioned "Uluru glowing deep red at dusk in the
Northern Territory", plus once more in the Northern Territory relocation guide as "Uluru at
sunset". Nobody had opened the file. It now lives at
`assets/act/act-parliament-house-front-elevation.jpg` under its real subject, and both uses point
at `nt-uluru-sunset.jpg`.

**Open the file before you caption it.** Two of the three ACT "parliament house" / "lake burley
griffin" files were also mislabelled when that folder was audited on 5 August. The filename is a
claim, not evidence.

## Verified genuine

- `nt-darwin.jpg` — Darwin's waterfront precinct and city towers from the air.
- `nt-an-aerial-view-of-darwin-2206700799.jpg` — Darwin from the air, wider.
- `nt-uluru-sunset.jpg` — genuine Uluru at sunset, wide panoramic. Good hero crop.
- `nt-uluru-australia-january-2020-panoramic-1779891818.jpg` — genuine Uluru, panoramic.

## Confirmed by Sophie, 23 Aug 2026 — now live

- `nt-darwin-marina-housing.jpg` — **Darwin**, confirmed. Marina-side housing. Live in the Darwin
  guide's suburbs chapter, which until now reused the city aerial.
- `nt-aboriginal-rock-art-sandstone.jpg` — **Kakadu**, confirmed. Live in the Darwin guide's
  weekends chapter. Rock art turned out to be a better subject for that slot than the waterfall it
  originally asked for: the chapter's Kakadu line already reads "some of the oldest rock art on
  earth", so photograph and copy now say the same thing.

Both were held back one turn pending confirmation rather than captioned on inference. That is the
process working — the Uluru error happened because nobody did this.

## Outstanding shot list

**Litchfield waterfalls.** Still nothing. A full sweep of all 557 site images on 23 Aug found five
files matching "falls": Whangārei Falls (NZ, genuine), Marokopa Falls (NZ), Russell Falls
(Tasmania, genuine — already used six times across the Hobart and Tasmania pages), and two of
Horizontal Falls in the WA Kimberley, which is a tidal race between cliffs, not a cascade. The one
Australian cascade now in the library is Queensland's. Litchfield is the shot worth commissioning:
ninety minutes from Darwin, and the drive Top End people actually do.
