Moving to Bunbury

A guide for healthcare professionals · Nine chapters

Western Australia’s largest regional city, two hours south of Perth, with a referral hospital, a university campus and three kinds of coastline inside the same city limits. This guide covers what is specific to Bunbury; everything national is explained once, elsewhere, and linked from here.

  • Internationally qualified clinicians weighing a move to the South West — from the UK, Ireland, South Africa, the UAE and beyond.
  • Our Australian work is currently concentrated in medical imaging, radiation therapy, nuclear medicine and radiology. If your profession is not one of those yet, we will say so plainly rather than waste your time.
