Medical imaging in Australia

An hour on what the move involves: Ahpra registration, what imaging roles look like across the public and private sectors, and the parts people wish they had asked about earlier.

Next date
To be announced — register to hear first
Time
An evening, UK, Ireland and South Africa time
Length
60 minutes
Where
Online · free

Who it is for

Radiographers, sonographers, radiation therapists, nuclear medicine technologists and radiologists who are thinking about Australia, whether that is this year or in two years’ time. You do not need to have made a decision, or started anything.

What we will cover

  • How Ahpra registration works, and the order to do things in
  • What the assessment asks of you, and where people lose months
  • How imaging services differ between the public and private sectors, and what that means for your week
  • Why pay is set state by state, and how to read an offer properly
  • Where the roles are, including the regional picture
  • What the move costs in the first year
Numbers are deliberately limited

We cap attendance so this stays a conversation rather than a broadcast. Everyone who comes should be able to ask their own question and get a straight answer, which does not work with two hundred people in the room. If it fills up we will run it again rather than raise the cap.

Who is running it

Sophie Careem, who founded Ethicare after twelve years in NHS service improvement and transformation, alongside the team who handle Australian registration day to day. Nothing you need to sign up to afterwards, and time for your questions.
