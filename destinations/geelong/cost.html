Cost of living

Housing is most of the story. Almost everything else in Geelong costs close to Melbourne prices, and a couple of things cost more — which is a more useful way to think about it than a single index number.

Why there are no dollar figures on this page

We publish rents and prices only where we have verified them against current data, and a figure that was true when a page was written is misleading a year later. Rather than print numbers we cannot stand behind, this chapter tells you the shape of the difference and where to check the current position yourself. For the national picture — tax, superannuation, Medicare, banking and what a first year actually costs — see Money, tax & banking in Australia.

What costs materially less

GeelongHousing — the largest single difference
MelbourneNoticeably higher to rent and to buy
SydneyA different order of magnitude

Relative widths are illustrative of direction, not measured values. Check current listings and the ABS or state data before you budget.

Housing, rented or bought
The reason many people move here and stay. What a clinical salary carries in Geelong is not comparable to what it carries in Melbourne, and the difference compounds over a career.
Parking and commuting
Generally easier and often free outside the CBD core, a real change from Melbourne’s inner suburbs.
Space for the money
A family home with a garden is realistic on a single clinical income in a way it increasingly is not in Melbourne.

What costs about the same

Groceries, fuel, insurance, utilities, phone and internet all sit close to Melbourne levels. Australia is a national market for most of this, and Geelong is a large enough city to have real competition. Do not expect a regional discount on the weekly shop; do not expect a penalty either.

What costs more

Coastal accommodation in summer
The Bellarine and Surf Coast see Melbourne-level holiday pricing from Boxing Day to late January, which is worth knowing if you are house-hunting in that window.
Anything with a small local market
Specialist trades, certain hobbies and niche services. Not dramatically, but the choice is narrower than Melbourne, and that shows in price.

The one-off costs of the first year

These are the ones people underestimate, and they are the same wherever in Australia you land: visa and registration fees, flights, shipping or replacing furniture, a car, a rental bond, and the gap between arriving and your first pay. Preparing to move covers the national picture, and our relocation cost calculator will give you a personal estimate.

Where to check the current position
  • Current rental listings for the specific suburbs in Where clinicians live — the fastest reality check there is.
  • State and ABS housing data for medians, rather than any figure on a recruiter’s website.
  • Ask us what a role actually pays before you model anything. We will tell you the framework and the range.
The short version

Treat Geelong as Melbourne prices with a materially cheaper roof over your head, plus a premium on coastal accommodation in peak summer. The housing difference is the entire argument, and it is a large one.
