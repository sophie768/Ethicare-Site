Professions

Every profession we cover

We recruit for a broad range of healthcare professions in New Zealand, and for medical imaging & radiation therapy in Australia — and we write guides for some professions we don’t yet place. Start from your profession rather than a country: each guide is the single home for that role (the regulator, the route and the work), whether or not we recruit into it today.

Find your role

Two different things sit on this page, and we label which is which. Most of these are professions we recruit into today. A few are professions we write about but do not yet place — the registration guidance is the same either way, and the family says so on its own line.

We would also rather cover a profession properly in one country than thinly in two. Where a role shows a single country, that is where we currently recruit into it — not an oversight.

If your profession is not here at all, it is still worth asking. We will tell you honestly whether we can help now, later, or not at all.

Whichever role you hold

Three things are true for every profession on this page, so we explain them once rather than on each guide.

Registration comes first, and it runs on its own clock

Every route starts with a regulator or a professional body deciding your qualification is comparable. That assessment is not ours to speed up, and it is the single most common reason a move takes longer than someone expected.

The honest advice is to start it before you start job-hunting seriously. Registration in New Zealand · Registration in Australia · Check your pathway

Pay is set by agreement, not negotiated role by role

New Zealand runs national collective agreements, so the same job pays the same wherever you do it. Australia runs state and territory enterprise agreements, so it does not — and superannuation sits on top of base.

Rough indicative bands sit on each profession’s guide, with the agreement named. Live vacancies say competitive, because the package is confirmed with you before you decide. The New Zealand agreements, verified

The rest of the move is the same for everyone

Visas, schools, housing, healthcare, banking, shipping, pets and the first few weeks do not change with your profession, so they live in one place instead of being repeated on every page.
