Moving to Geelong

A guide for healthcare professionals · Nine chapters

Victoria’s second city, an hour from Melbourne, with the only tertiary hospital in the state based outside the capital and the Great Ocean Road starting at its edge. This guide covers what is specific to Geelong; everything national is explained once, elsewhere, and linked from here.
