# WA photography — audit

## Added 23 Aug 2026

- `wa-perth-skyline-from-kings-park.jpg` — the Perth CBD from Kings Park in late afternoon light:
  the lookout path and native beds in the foreground, a family with a pram on the path, the towers
  and the Swan beyond. **Perth, confirmed** — the BHP sign on the blue tower and Central Park's
  white spire are both legible in frame, and the vantage is the Kings Park escarpment.
  The first Perth photograph that puts people in it. The others are skyline and coast; this one is
  someone walking a pram after work, which is closer to what a relocating family is actually asking
  about.

- `wa-busselton-jetty-aerial.jpg` — the Busselton jetty from the air: the long timber pile jetty
  running out over turquoise water, a netted swimming enclosure alongside, Norfolk Island pines and
  the low town behind, the Vasse estuary beyond. **Busselton, confirmed** — the jetty is the longest
  timber-piled jetty in the southern hemisphere and hard to mistake, and the estuary behind the town
  matches. Relevant to us: our WA vacancies sit in the South West (Bunbury, Mandurah), and this is
  the region's best-known landmark.

## Note on the two "falls" files

`wa-horizontal-falls.jpg` and `kimberley-horizontal-falls.jpg` are the same subject —
Horizontal Falls in the Kimberley — at two sizes (1500px / 3840px), so they are effectively
duplicates. Worth keeping only the large one. Also worth knowing that despite the name it is **not
a waterfall**: it is a tidal race squeezing between two cliffs. Do not use it to illustrate a
cascade.
