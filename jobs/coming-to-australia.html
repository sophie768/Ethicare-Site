Australia · what we’re building next

More Australian professions are in development

We opened in Australia with medical imaging because we understand it deeply — the registration, the pay, the questions people ask. We’re building the same depth for more professions, rather than publishing thin pages just to say they’re here.

We’re starting with what we know really well

In New Zealand we recruit across most of healthcare. Australia is younger for us — we opened here in March 2026 — so we’re growing it one profession at a time, and we’d rather tell you where each one stands than pretend it’s ready.

Live now: medical imaging & radiation — sonography, radiography, radiation therapy, nuclear medicine and radiology, each with a full pathway, indicative pay and the picture of the move.

Explore medical imaging & radiation →Check my registration pathway
In the works

In development right now

Each of these is a different registration picture — often more than one regulator inside a single family. That’s exactly why we’re taking the time: a guide that gets the route wrong costs you months.

  • Mental healthIn development

    Psychology, psychiatry and the wider team — each answering to a different regulator and a different route. Worth getting right rather than quick.

  • Theatre & perioperativeIn development

    Scrub, scout, anaesthetic and recovery roles across public and private lists. The registration picture differs by role, so we’re building it role by role.

  • Allied healthIn development

    Physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech pathology and more — several regulators, several routes. A broad family, and we’re taking it one profession at a time.

Not sure your route even works?Our pathway checker already covers more than twenty professions — well beyond the ones we recruit into — so you can get a clear answer on registration today, whether or not the full guide is live.

Interested in working in Australia?

Tell us, and we’ll be in touch nearer the time

For roles in Australia we are focusing on medical imaging at present. That will change in the coming weeks. If you are interested in working in Australia, leave your profession and an email and we will contact you with more information nearer the time — and what you tell us shapes the order we build the guides in.

Don’t wait for the page

The guide isn’t ready — we might still be able to help

A webpage being unfinished doesn’t mean we can’t be useful. If you’re seriously considering Australia, a conversation costs nothing and there’s never any pressure in one.

Two things you can do today

Get a straight answer on registration. The pathway checker covers most professions already, so you can see the route that’s likely to apply to you before you spend a penny.

Talk it through with a person. Tell us where you are and what you’re weighing up. We’ll be honest about whether and how we can help right now — and if we can’t yet, we’ll say so.
