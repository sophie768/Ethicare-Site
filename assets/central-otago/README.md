# Central Otago photography — audit

## Added 23 Aug 2026

- `queenstown-hillside-housing-street.jpg` — an ordinary residential street climbing a hillside:
  houses of every era and roof colour stepped up the slope, pines and gorse above them, a schist
  mountain behind, cars parked along the kerb, a road with a real centre line. Queenstown, on
  Sophie's attribution; **New Zealand confirmed independently** by the NZ plates on two vehicles in
  the foreground (TH828, CSZ647).

  **The most useful kind of photograph we can add, and the rarest.** The shot list has been asking
  for ordinary residential streets since the audit: they are the hardest thing to buy as stock,
  because nobody photographs them on purpose, and they are what a destination page is actually for.
  This one answers "what would my street look like" — which no lake or mountain view can.

  Note what makes it work: it is not tidy. Mixed housing stock, gorse on the hillside, a No Large
  Passenger Vehicle Parking sign, dusty 4WDs. That is the honest register the guides are written in,
  and it beats a real-estate-style shot of a new build.

  One caveat for captioning: Queenstown is a resort town and among the most expensive places to buy
  in New Zealand, so do **not** use this as a generic "NZ suburban living" image on a national page
  or next to affordability copy. Keep it in the Central Otago guide, where the local cost picture is
  stated plainly alongside it.
