# Christchurch photography — audit

## Added 23 Aug 2026

- `christchurch-lyttelton-harbour-sailgp.jpg` — Lyttelton Harbour from above: a fleet of SailGP F50
  foiling catamarans mid-race on turquoise water, spectator boats and moored yachts beyond, a
  hillside settlement on the headland, the dry volcanic rim of Banks Peninsula behind.
  **Lyttelton, confirmed** — the crater-rim landform, the harbour basin and the F50 fleet together
  place it beyond doubt; Christchurch has hosted SailGP on this water.

  Worth having because Lyttelton is the part of Christchurch that surprises people. The city reads
  flat and rebuilt from the centre; twenty minutes over (or through) the hill there is a working
  port, a crater harbour and hillside villages. For a clinician picturing Christchurch as a plain on
  a plain, this is the corrective.

  Two captioning notes. Do not name the settlement — it reads as Diamond Harbour or Church Bay, but
  that is inference from the landform, not evidence. And SailGP is a visiting event, not everyday
  life: caption it as the harbour, with the racing incidental, or it overstates. Tall portrait crop
  (1500×2250).
