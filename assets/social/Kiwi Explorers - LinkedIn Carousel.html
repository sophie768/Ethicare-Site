Not just a relocation pack

A six-year-old deserves her own guide

One of the anaesthetic technicians we are moving to Wellington flies ahead of his family. His daughter is six. She has been told she is leaving the only country she has ever known, and everything sent to the house so far has been addressed to her dad.

So we wrote one for her.

Things to find in her first week

A pīwakawakaA tiny fantail. It follows you along the path.
A tūīA shiny bird with a white throat and a loud voice.
The red cable carIt climbs the hill above the city centre.
A silver fernTurn the leaf over. The underneath is silvery.
A koruA curled-up baby fern, like a little spiral.
JandalsWhat flip-flops are called here.
Wind that pushes youWellington is famous for it. Lean on it and laugh.
A big ferryBoats that carry cars all the way to the South Island.

Birds, cable cars, ferries, ferns. New words, new places.
