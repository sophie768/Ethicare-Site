# Ethicare Resourcing — what the site still needs, after the image audit

Sixty-one chapters have no photograph (healthcare chapters excluded — they carry the
brand icon by design). They are not spread evenly: the gap is concentrated in the three
chapter types that call for ordinary, unglamorous pictures.

## The same brief as before

- At least 2000 pixels wide, landscape. Portrait shots are welcome too — they sit beside text.
- Real places on ordinary days. Daylight, people living their lives, nothing staged.
- **No hospitals, clinics, or anything carrying a health service's name or logo.**
- Avoid number plates, close-up faces without a release, and brand signage as the subject.
- Each photo is used once. No near-identical shots of the same view.
- We need the right to use it on the website and in the printed guides.

## Getting around — 17 chapters, the biggest gap

Adelaide · Bay of Plenty · Brisbane · Bunbury · Bundaberg · Darwin · Dunedin · Geelong ·
Gisborne · Mandurah · Nelson-Tasman · New South Wales · Palmerston North ·
South Australia · Tasmania · Victoria · Waikato

**What works:** a train or tram at a platform, a bus on an ordinary street, a person cycling
to work, a highway out of town, an airport terminal, a bridge with traffic on it.

**What doesn't:** anything scenic. Seven of these chapters previously carried a park, a
fountain, a museum or a beach, and I have taken those down rather than leave them
implying something the chapter isn't about.

## Cost of living — 10 chapters

Adelaide · Canberra · Central Otago · Darwin · Gisborne · Melbourne · Northland ·
South Australia · Southland · Victoria

**What works:** the housing stock itself — a street of ordinary homes, an apartment block,
a rental sign; or everyday spending — a supermarket aisle, a weekly shop, a suburban
shopping centre.

**What doesn't:** marinas, vineyards, beaches. They say the opposite of what the chapter says.

## Where clinicians live — 4 chapters

Brisbane · Melbourne · South Australia · Southland

**What works:** a residential street, front gardens, a row of houses, a school run, a local
shopping strip. Ordinary and tidy beats aspirational.

## Three guides that need a different kind of photo, not more of the same

These are illustrated, but almost every picture is the coast:

- **Newcastle** — six of nine chapters show a beach. It needs the city: the streets, the
  houses, the working harbour.
- **Gold Coast** — six of eight are beach or skyline. It needs suburbs and everyday life.
- **Mandurah** — five of eight are shore or dunes. Same.

## If the budget only stretches to twelve

Take the six busiest transport gaps — Adelaide, Brisbane, Melbourne, Darwin, Tasmania,
Victoria — and six cost-of-living housing shots: Adelaide, Canberra, Melbourne, Darwin,
Southland, Victoria. Those twelve close the two gaps that currently look most like
something is missing.
