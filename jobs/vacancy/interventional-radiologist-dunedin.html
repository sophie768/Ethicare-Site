Consultant Interventional Radiologist — Dunedin

PermanentFull-timeDunedin, New Zealand

Ethicare is delighted to be working with Health New Zealand | Te Whatu Ora to recruit a Consultant Interventional Radiologist in Dunedin. This is a fantastic opportunity for an interventional radiologist to join a close, collegial team of ten and take on a broad range of vascular and non-vascular work.

About the role

You’ll join a radiology service of around ten (general radiologists and four other interventional radiologists) providing imaging to roughly 310,000 people across the lower South Island, with predominantly emergency and inpatient work. A full range of modalities runs on a digital RIS/PACS: MRI, interventional, CT and ultrasound (mammography is not provided).

It’s a relaxed, informal, first-name team that works as a strong MDT, with the close collegial relationships you only get in a smaller hospital. You’ll often hear how your patients got on after they left your care.

What the role involves

  • A wide range of techniques: peripheral vascular, biliary drainage and embolisation, nephrostomy insertion, trauma embolisation, tunnelled IV access and fistula intervention
  • EVAR/TEVAR procedures, with the opportunity to be involved if you wish
  • Contributing to the general radiology component and the out-of-hours roster
  • Supporting service and quality initiatives, and teaching junior staff

About you

  • FRANZCR (or equivalent), eligible for vocational registration with the Medical Council of New Zealand
  • A subspecialty in interventional radiology, able to join the vascular interventional daytime and on-call roster (general call optional)
  • An outstanding team player, enthusiastic about teaching
  • International candidates are warmly encouraged to apply

About Dunedin

Dunedin is a warm, walkable university city in the south, with a strong sense of community, excellent schools and a lively arts scene, surrounded by coastline, wildlife and mountains, with Central Otago, Queenstown and Fiordland only a few hours away.
