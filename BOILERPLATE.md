# How Ethicare describes itself

One canonical wording for each place Ethicare is described from the outside, so the same
company turns up in each of them. Agreed 1 October 2026.

Why a file: a boilerplate that lives only in someone's head gets improvised, and after a year
the LinkedIn page, the Google listing and the footer are three different companies. Change it
here, then change it everywhere it is used — the list at the foot of this file says where.

---

## The paragraph

For LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, directory and partner listings, press and pitch
material — anywhere there is no site design and no Sophie to carry it.

> Ethicare Resourcing helps healthcare professionals find their next role and make a new home
> in New Zealand or Australia.
>
> A move like this is about more than a job — your future, the people you love, the life you
> are trying to build. So we tell you what it costs, what it pays, how long registration takes,
> and when the numbers do not add up.
>
> The tools and guides are free and open, whether your job comes from us or not. We never
> charge a candidate a fee.

**Why it is written this way.** The first sentence says what Ethicare does, plainly, because
that is the thing people most often cannot find. The second proves the care rather than
asserting it — "we believe everyone deserves clear information" is what every agency says and
what no reader can check. The third is the part competitors cannot copy without changing their
business.

## The homepage meta description (already live)

What Google prints under the name in search results. 159 characters; Google cuts off around 160.

> Helping healthcare professionals find their next role and make a new home in New Zealand or
> Australia. Free tools, honest guidance, never a fee to a candidate.

---

## What must stay true in any version

- **New Zealand: multiple professions. Australia: medical imaging, radiation therapy, nuclear
  medicine and radiology.** Never imply the Australian scope is wider than it is.
- **Never a fee to a candidate.** The employer pays.
- **The tools are free and open**, whether or not the role comes from Ethicare. This is the
  claim the whole site rests on; everything else is negotiable.
- **No numbers without a source.** Placement counts, retention rates and timescales do not
  belong in boilerplate unless they can be evidenced on the day they are read.
- Ethicare is not a union, and does not speak for a regulator or for APEX.
