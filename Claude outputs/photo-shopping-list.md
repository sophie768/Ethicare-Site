# Ethicare Resourcing — photo shopping list

Forty photos, five for each of the eight thinnest destination guides. Getting these
would lift every guide to roughly the level of Perth, Sydney and Christchurch.

## What every photo needs

- **Size:** at least 2000 pixels wide, landscape. A few portrait shots are welcome too — they sit beside text in the middle of a chapter.
- **Feel:** real places on ordinary days. Daylight, people living their lives, nothing staged or heavily filtered.
- **Must not include:** hospitals, clinics or anything with a health service's name or logo on it. Only Health New Zealand · Te Whatu Ora may be named anywhere on the site.
- **Also avoid:** number plates, faces in close-up without a release, and brand signage as the subject.
- **Each photo is used once.** Nothing repeats anywhere on the site, so please avoid near-identical shots of the same view.
- **Licence:** we need the right to use it on the website and in the printed guides.

## If the budget only stretches to ten

Take the first two from each of the four thinnest guides: Southland, Darwin,
Hawke's Bay and Queensland. Then Hobart's mountain view and the Bay of Plenty
waterfront. Those ten remove the longest runs of photo-free chapters.
