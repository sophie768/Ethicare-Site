Mammographer — Bundaberg, Queensland

PermanentFull-timeBundaberg, Queensland, Australia

Ethicare is delighted to be supporting a values-led private medical imaging provider in Bundaberg to recruit an experienced Mammographer. It’s a chance to combine a rewarding career with an exceptional coastal lifestyle, in a modern breast imaging service known for clinical excellence, collaboration and putting people first.

About the role

Working as part of an experienced multidisciplinary imaging team, you’ll perform high-quality screening and diagnostic mammography while helping to deliver an exceptional patient experience. You’ll be valued not only for your clinical expertise, but for the compassionate care you bring to every patient.

The service is equipped with modern technology and committed to continuous improvement, professional development and the highest standards of clinical care, led by an approachable, values-driven team.

What the role involves

  • Performing high-quality screening and diagnostic mammography
  • Delivering an exceptional, compassionate patient experience
  • Working within an experienced multidisciplinary imaging team
  • Using modern breast-imaging technology in a service committed to continuous improvement

About you

  • Current Ahpra registration as a Medical Radiation Practitioner — or an Ahpra application already submitted and eligible for registration
  • Experience in mammography
  • Excellent communication and patient-care skills
  • A collaborative, team-focused approach
  • A genuine commitment to high-quality, compassionate care

Why join?

  • A friendly, supportive and values-led organisation
  • An experienced and welcoming clinical team
  • Modern equipment and high-quality imaging facilities
  • Ongoing professional development and career growth
  • A competitive salary, confirmed with you before you decide
  • Relocation support provided

About Bundaberg

Bundaberg offers an outstanding quality of life: beautiful beaches, a warm climate and a relaxed coastal lifestyle, with the perfect balance between career and time outdoors, from the Southern Great Barrier Reef to nearby national parks and quiet weekends by the ocean. With affordable housing, excellent schools and a welcoming community, it’s an ideal place for individuals, couples and families looking for a lifestyle change.
