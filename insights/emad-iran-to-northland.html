From Iran to Northland

A regular hiker, an anaesthetic technician, and a New Zealand town he had never heard of.

Emad Kabiri · Anaesthetic technicianIran → Whāngārei, NorthlandPhotographs by Emad Kabiri

Emad Kabiri moved from Iran to New Zealand to work as an Anaesthetic Technician. Today he works for Health New Zealand in Northland, a region he had not heard of until we suggested it.

“I had never heard of Whāngārei before Ethicare mentioned it to me.”

Emad
Why Northland

A hiker, looking at the map

“I was a regular hiker back home in Iran, so once I started looking into Northland, it immediately appealed to me.”

Emad
The work

A good mix of cases

“I now work as an Anaesthetic Technician for Health New Zealand in Northland, where we see a good mix of general and elective surgery. The bigger and more complex cases tend to be treated in Auckland or other major centres.”

Emad
Life outside work

Beaches, forests and coastal walks

“Northland is absolutely beautiful. I love hiking and photography, so having beaches, forests, coastal walks and incredible scenery so close by is a real highlight.”

Emad
The honest bit

Much quieter than he was used to

“Whāngārei is much quieter than I was used to. If you’re looking for big-city life and nightlife, it probably isn’t for you. But for families or anyone who enjoys the outdoors and a laid-back beach lifestyle, it’s a great place to live.”

Emad
Looking back

Somewhere he might never have considered

“I’m really pleased Ethicare introduced me to somewhere I might never have considered on my own.”

Emad
