# ACT photography — MISLABELLED FILES, DO NOT USE AS CANBERRA

Audited 5 August 2026 by opening every file. Two are **not Canberra** despite their filenames:

- **`act-lake-burley-griffin.jpg`** — a coastal aerial: turquoise ocean, surf beach, headland, high-rise
  strip. Almost certainly the Gold Coast / Tweed Heads area. Canberra is inland and has no coast.
  Was live on the ACT guide captioned "Lake Burley Griffin sits at the heart of Australia's planned
  capital." Removed.
- **`act-drone-aerial-view-of-beautiful-2348624325.jpg`** — a white-sand bay beach backed by dense
  coastal forest, turquoise water. A NSW south-coast beach (Jervis Bay area), not Canberra. Was live
  on the ACT guide four times, captioned "Aerial view over Canberra", "Canberra from above",
  "A leafy Canberra district" and "Lakeside paths … getting around Canberra easy." Removed.

Neither is referenced by any live page. Do not re-use them for Canberra. If their true locations are
confirmed they could be renamed and used for the Gold Coast and NSW south coast respectively.
