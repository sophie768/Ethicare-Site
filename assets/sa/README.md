# SA photography — audit

## Added 23 Aug 2026

- `sa-adelaide-canal-beachside-suburb-aerial.jpg` — a beachside suburb from the air: large houses
  with pools and jetties along a still canal or lake edge, the suburb behind them, and the open sea
  on the far side. Filed on Sophie's attribution, hedged ("Adelaide and Busselton I think").
  **Plausible but not independently confirmed.** It fits Adelaide's West Lakes closely — a man-made
  lake sitting immediately behind a beachfront strip on Gulf St Vincent, which is an unusual
  arrangement. But canal estates at Mandurah (WA) and Patterson Lakes (VIC) look much the same from
  a drone, and there is no signage or landmark in frame.
  **Caption it for the housing type, not the suburb** — waterfront housing with the beach on the
  other side of the street — until someone who knows Adelaide confirms it. If it is West Lakes it is
  a useful one to have: it shows the top of the market, which is worth being honest about alongside
  the cost-of-living numbers.
