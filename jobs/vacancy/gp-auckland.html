General Practice · Primary care

General practice, with time for your patients

PermanentFull-timePart-timeAuckland, New Zealand

Ethicare Resourcing is working with a well-established primary healthcare provider in Auckland to recruit experienced General Practitioners interested in building their career and their life in New Zealand.

About the role

This is an opportunity to join a supportive, multidisciplinary primary care environment working with diverse communities across Auckland. The organisation has an established network of medical centres and a strong focus on accessible, high-quality community healthcare.

As a GP you will provide comprehensive primary care across a broad range of presentations, working alongside experienced GPs, nurses and wider primary healthcare teams. It is the kind of setting where you build long-term relationships with patients and, over time, become part of the local community rather than a passing face.

Depending on your experience and interests, there may also be opportunities to work across different Auckland locations.

What the role involves

  • Comprehensive primary care across a broad range of presentations
  • Working within an established multidisciplinary team of GPs, nurses and wider primary healthcare staff
  • Continuity of care and long-term patient relationships
  • Scope to work across different Auckland locations, depending on your interests

About you

We would like to hear from GPs who hold a recognised medical qualification and are genuinely considering a move to New Zealand. Applications are welcomed from both New Zealand-based and internationally trained doctors.

  • A recognised medical qualification
  • Solid post-qualification experience in General Practice or Family Medicine
  • Eligible, or potentially eligible, for registration with the Medical Council of New Zealand
  • Comfortable working with patients from diverse cultural and socioeconomic backgrounds
  • Strong communication skills and a patient-centred approach

Why consider Auckland?

Auckland offers the advantages of a major international city while giving you easy access to beaches, regional parks, islands and some of New Zealand’s best-known coastal areas. For doctors relocating with families, the city also offers a wide choice of neighbourhoods and schools, alongside established international communities.

Relocating from overseas?

Moving countries involves considerably more than accepting a job. Ethicare supports international doctors through the whole process: initial eligibility discussions, interview preparation, registration guidance, immigration and visa coordination, and practical relocation support.

We will also talk you through the realities of working and living in New Zealand before you make a decision. Our aim is not simply to secure you a role, but to help you make an informed move that works for you and your family.

Interested?

If you are an experienced GP considering New Zealand, we would be very happy to have an initial confidential conversation. You do not need to have completed New Zealand registration before getting in touch — we can assess your background first and help you understand the likely pathway.
