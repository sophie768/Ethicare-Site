Getting ready to apply

Are you ready to apply?

You are almost certainly more prepared than you think. What catches experienced clinicians out is rarely the clinical questions — it is a CV written for a different system, a referee nobody can reach, and being asked for an example on the spot.

This is the readiness check. Our CV and interview guide explains the how — this tells you what is still outstanding. Everything you tick or write stays on this device.

Applying to
Your examples, ready

Eight examples worth having ready

Nearly every healthcare interview here asks for real examples, and thinking of one on the spot is where good clinicians stumble. Set each one out in three parts, because that is the order a panel hears it in, the situation, what you personally did, and how it ended. A few words per part is enough; you are jogging your own memory, not writing a script.

Saved on this device only. Print the page to take them into the interview with you.

Question bank

Questions you can expect

What panels here ask, with a note on what a strong answer covers. Flag one as needing work and a worked example appears, set out in its parts, so you can see the shape of an answer rather than the words of one.

Answer each one out loud, then rate yourself honestly. Anything you flag becomes your agenda at the bottom of the page.

You can write an answer out too. Nothing marks it and nothing rewrites it — a panel can hear a rehearsed script, and your own example is the thing that lands. The four checks under each box are what a panel is listening for. Leave out anything that could identify a patient.

What comes next

If you receive an offer, it needs reading properly.
