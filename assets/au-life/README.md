# au-life — national/everyday imagery audit

## Reef photography — CANNOT be attributed to a named reef (23 Aug 2026)

- `reef-turtle-anemonefish.jpg` — a green turtle over coral with pink anemonefish and an anemone.
- `reef-snorkel-fusiliers.jpg` — yellowtail fusiliers just under the surface, coral below.
  Vertical crop, good on a phone.

Both are genuine tropical-reef photographs and both are **unattributable from the pixels**. There is
no landmark underwater: the species in these frames occur right across the Indo-Pacific, so the
same photograph could honestly be the Great Barrier Reef, Ningaloo, or somewhere outside Australia
entirely. **Safe as generic tropical-reef imagery. NOT safe captioned "the Great Barrier Reef"
without provenance from the supplier** — and specifically never as Ningaloo on a WA page, which
would be the Uluru error with a different subject.

Note that the existing `great-barrier-reef.jpg` in this folder carries exactly the kind of name
that cannot be verified by looking at it. Treat its caption with the same caution until someone
confirms the source.
