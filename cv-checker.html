CV checker

Check the CV you already have

Most clinicians find out their CV does not read well in New Zealand or Australia after they have been turned down, and nobody tells them why. This tells you first. Paste it in and you will see what a hiring manager here would notice, in the order they would notice it.

Ten checks for every CV, and up to four more for your profession. None of them is a score. It is your CV, and you are welcome to disagree with any of it.

The destination changes a few checks, because the regulators and the vocabulary do. Your profession adds the clinical detail employers in it scan for: modalities, therapeutic approaches, equipment by name.

Your CV

Paste the whole thing

Open your CV, select all of it, copy, and paste it below. Formatting does not matter and headings are welcome — several of the checks go looking for them.

Nothing pasted yet.

Nothing is uploaded and nothing is saved. The text is read in this browser tab and is gone the moment you close it or clear the box. We never see it, so nothing you paste here reaches a recruiter until you decide to send it.

One thing pasted text cannot show us is a photograph. If your CV has one, take it off: neither country expects one, and it is the quickest way to look unfamiliar with local convention.

What a reader would notice

Your results

Ordered the way a hiring manager reads: what stops them first, then what they would query, then what already works. Nothing here is a score.

How these fit together

Three separate things, in this order

Checking a CV, building one and registering with us are three different steps, and you do not need all three. The first two are yours to keep; only the third involves us.

  1. 01

    Check the CV you have

    Two minutes, if you already have a CV. It tells you what a reader here would notice and leaves you to decide what to change.

    You are here
  2. 02

    Or write it properly, once

    About twenty minutes. Nine sections in the order a reader wants them, building an A4 page as you type, and a Word file at the end that you keep editing for every application.

    Open the CV builder →
  3. 03

    Then register with us

    A separate step, and about three minutes. Tell us where you are up to and attach the CV file. Neither tool sends anything to us on its own, so registering is the only way we see it.

    Register and send your CV →

Then the parts a CV cannot do

A good CV gets you the interview, not the job. Registration is its own process and starts earlier than most people expect, and the interview is where the claims on the page get tested.
